    Built-in models¶

    These model plugins ship with the platform (tier T0). They register through the mapi.models entry point, so default_registry() and the control-plane catalog list them without any configuration. Every one passes the contract kit (mapi_sdk.testing.check_model) and has a conformance fixture (see Conformance corpus).

    Plugin Package Representation Score modes Corpus fixture
    mmm.hierarchical_geo@1 mapi-plugin-mmm draws predict, forecast, counterfactual, simulate, evaluate e33
    mmm.pymc_marketing@1 mapi-plugin-mmm draws predict, forecast, counterfactual, evaluate pymc_marketing
    ts.hsgp_trend@1 mapi-plugin-ts draws predict, forecast, evaluate c08
    ts.gaussian_hmm@1 mapi-plugin-ts draws predict, forecast, filter, evaluate e24
    tabular.hierarchical_glm@1 mapi-plugin-tabular draws predict, counterfactual, evaluate e02
    tabular.regressor@1 mapi-plugin-tabular quantiles predict, forecast, counterfactual, evaluate xgb_store_demand

    Plugins use canonical names for their inputs; bind your dataset's names onto them in the spec's data.bind block. Outputs come back under your names (reverse binding) unless they are new variables such as y_hat.

    The mapi-sklearn package also registers five general-purpose tabular plugins, sklearn.tabular_regressor, sklearn.tabular_quantiles, sklearn.tabular_bootstrap, sklearn.tabular_conformal and sklearn.tabular_classifier; see Tabular models.

    Every setting, generated

    The models catalog is generated from the installed manifests: capabilities, outputs, derived quantities, every setting with its default and Guided-mode level, and the input data contract.

    mmm.hierarchical_geo: geo marketing mix¶

    A hierarchical marketing-mix model: adstock over a core.lag_tensor spend tensor, logistic or Hill saturation, channel effects partially pooled across geos, a hierarchical intercept and controls. New geos are drawn from the fitted hyperprior. Contributions are additive and, with outputs.derived, the fit caches contributions, per-draw response curves and marginal costs in original units. It declares a decision objective for budget optimisation.

    Inputs. y(geo, time), spend(geo, channel, time, lag) from core.lag_tensor (the adstock.l_max setting must equal the step's l_max), and any other numeric variable along time as a control. geo is new_levels (hyperprior), channel is fixed, time extends.

    Outputs. y_hat(geo, time) and contributions(component, geo, time) (one component per channel, baseline and one per control; they sum to y_hat).

    Read the marketing-mix guide for the model, its priors and a complete spec.

    mmm.pymc_marketing: the pymc-marketing adapter¶

    Runs pymc_marketing.mmm.MMM behind the platform. The model scales data, carries spend over and reports original units itself, so the plugin declares owns: [scaling, carryover, inverse_units] and the platform skips its own versions of those. Bundles store the posterior, pymc-marketing's fit_data and its JSON model description, never a pickle. Markets are a fixed dim: pymc-marketing cannot score a market it has not seen. See the adapter section.

    ts.hsgp_trend — smooth trend with seasonality¶

    A Hilbert-space Gaussian process (HSGP) trend plus an optional linear drift and Fourier seasonality, for one regularly spaced series (Mauna-Loa style CO2, sales, traffic).

    Inputs. t (time, policy extend) and the target y(t). Missing months are allowed: replace sentinel codes with core.replace_sentinel and they are left out of the likelihood while the model still interpolates them. Put core.standardize on y; the priors are on the standardised scale and outputs are mapped back to your units.

    Forecast horizon. max_extension (default 24 periods) is part of the model's input schema. The HSGP domain is sized at fit time from the anticipated coordinates (the fitted range plus max_extension periods), so every forecast up to that horizon evaluates exactly the fitted functions. A longer horizon is rejected before any compute with HORIZON_EXCEEDS_FIT (HTTP 409): refit with a larger max_extension.

    Setting Default Meaning
    max_extension 24 Periods a forecast may reach past the fit without a refit
    kernel expquad expquad, matern52 or matern32
    m, c 30, 1.5 Basis functions and boundary factor of the approximation
    lengthscale_mu, lengthscale_sigma 0.4, 0.5 Log-normal lengthscale prior on the scaled axis [-1, 1]
    eta_sigma 1.0 Half-normal scale of the GP amplitude
    linear_trend true Add intercept + slope * t
    season_period, season_order 12, 3 Fourier seasonality (periods of t, order)
    noise_sigma 0.5 Half-normal scale of the observation noise

    Outputs. y_hat(t) (expected value or a predictive draw) and trend(t), the long-term component without seasonality, both in the target's units.

    data:
  bind: {dims: {t: date}, vars: {y: co2}}
  split: {kind: time_holdout, dim: t, last: 12}
pipeline:
  - {id: clean, use: core.replace_sentinel@^1, with: {vars: [y], below: 0}}
  - {id: scale, use: core.standardize@^1, with: {vars: [y], over: [t]}}
model:
  use: ts.hsgp_trend@^1
  settings: {max_extension: 24, season_order: 2}
  fit: {mode: evaluate_then_refit, engine: pymc, seed: 11}

    ts.gaussian_hmm — hidden states with a terminal state¶

    A K-state Gaussian hidden Markov model over many sequences at once (animal tracks, machine regimes, customer phases). Hidden states are summed out with the forward algorithm, so sampling is fast and exact.

    Inputs. Padded layout: animal (the sequence dim, new_levels with strategy pooled), step (extend), y(animal, step) with NaN padding and a boolean mask(animal, step).

    Terminal state. After the fit the filtered state distribution at the last fitted step is stored as the bundle's terminal_state group, alpha_T(chain, draw, animal, state).

    • predict / evaluate use the one-step-ahead state distribution of the fit inside the fitted range and alpha_T propagated by the transition matrix after it; they never look at targets in the payload.
    • forecast propagates alpha_T over the horizon.
    • filter runs the forward filter over new observations that continue the fitted steps and returns an updated terminal_state group with the filtered state_prob.
    • New animals start from the initial distribution pi at the first fitted step.

    Draws are relabelled after sampling so that the state means increase (label switching); identification: ordered uses an ordered prior instead. Pointwise LOO is declared invalid (elpd_loo is rejected at validation): steps within a track are dependent.

    Setting Default Meaning
    n_states 2 Number of hidden states
    mu_center, mu_sigma 0.0, 2.0 Normal prior of the state means
    sigma_scale 1.0 Half-normal scale of the per-state sds
    stickiness 2.0 Extra Dirichlet weight on staying in a state
    pi_concentration 1.0 Dirichlet weight of the initial distribution
    identification relabel relabel (sort draws by mean) or ordered

    Outputs. y_hat(animal, step) in your units and state_prob(animal, step, state) (units probability, never inverse-transformed).

    tabular.hierarchical_glm — partial pooling in long layout¶

    Varying intercepts (and optionally slopes) by group with group-level predictors: the radon model and its many relatives (schools, stores, clinics).

    Inputs. Long layout: rows along obs (extend), a group dim (group_dim, default county, new_levels with strategy hyperprior), the index <group_dim>_idx(obs), the target y(obs), observation-level covariates and group-level group_covariates. Covariates are part of the input schema, so a spec naming a missing one fails validation.

    New groups. A county the model never saw is drawn from the fitted hyperprior for every posterior draw; its group-level covariates shift its prior mean exactly as for fitted counties. Score payloads may list counties in any order: the platform remaps county_idx.

    Setting Default Meaning
    group_dim county The grouping dim
    family normal normal, student_t, poisson (log link) or bernoulli (logit)
    covariates [] Predictors over obs
    varying_slopes [] Covariates whose slope varies by group
    group_covariates [] Predictors over the group dim
    centered false Centred group effects

    tabular.regressor — quantile regression for panels¶

    The plan's store-demand model: rows are stacked from labelled dims (store × sku × week), the coordinates become categorical features, and an allow-listed estimator predicts conditional quantiles. Its estimator setting accepts exactly the quantile-capable estimators of the registry (xgboost, scikit-learn's gradient boosting and histogram boosting, and quantile regression), so other estimators are rejected at validation.

    • Defaults: xgboost (300 trees, depth 5, learning rate 0.05) and quantiles [0.1, 0.5, 0.9].
    • New stores are pooled (new_levels: pooled); forecasting is direct, so future covariates marked future_required must be supplied (MISSING_FUTURE_COVARIATE otherwise).
    • Quantiles are per cell: aggregating them across stores is refused (CAPABILITY_UNSUPPORTED).
    • The manifest lists core.fit_vs_actual, core.residuals_over_time and ml.feature_importance.

    All other settings are those of mapi_sklearn (see Tabular models).
